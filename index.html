About University of ibadan✨

The University of Ibadan (UI), Nigeria’s premier university, has been a beacon of learning, research, and innovation since its establishment in 1948. Originally founded as a college under the University of London, UI became an independent university in 1962, and today, it stands as one of the most respected institutions of higher learning in Africa.

Located in Ibadan, Oyo State, the University of Ibadan offers a rich academic environment where tradition meets modernity. With a sprawling campus, state-of-the-art facilities, and a vibrant student community, UI is designed to nurture intellectual growth, creativity, and leadership.

Our Faculties💫

UI is home to 17 faculties and more than 90 departments, covering fields from the sciences to arts, social sciences, health sciences, and law. Some of the most prominent faculties include:

  • Faculty of Arts – nurturing creativity and critical thinking
  • Faculty of Science – driving innovation in research and technology
  • College of Medicine – training world-class medical professionals
  • Faculty of Education – shaping future educators and leaders
  • Faculty of Law – producing some of Nigeria’s finest legal minds

Why UI Stands out🥰

  • Oldest university in Nigeria with a rich heritage
  • A diverse and inclusive campus, welcoming students from across Nigeria and beyond
  • Cutting-edge research and innovation, contributing to national and global knowledge
  • Vibrant student life with clubs, societies, and cultural activities
  • A commitment to excellence, inspiring generations of leaders

Campus Life🤭

UI offers more than academics — from sports and cultural events to clubs, mentoring programs, and social initiatives, students experience holistic development. A virtual tour or video of the campus can help you get a feel of student life and facilities before you even arrive.

UI Hostels: Your Home on Campus

Living on campus is one of the most exciting parts of university life at University of Ibadan. The hostels provide a safe, convenient, and social environment for students.

Female Hostels🥺

  1. Queen Elizabeth II Hall (Queens Hall)🤭

    2. One of the oldest and largest female hostels. Known for its strong traditions and welcoming community. Offers shared rooms with basic furniture and common facilities.

  2. Queen Idia Hall

    3. A serene and well-managed female hostel. Neat, secure, and comfortable with convenient access to campus amenities. Popular among freshers for its calm and friendly atmosphere.

Male Hostels

  1. Independence Hall (Indy Hall)

    2. A popular male hostel for undergraduates. Lively hall culture with multiple blocks of shared rooms. Close to lecture theatres and student services.

  2. Mellanby Hall

    3. Traditional male hostel with historic significance. Known for strong hall traditions and community life.

  3. Tedder Hall

    4. Male hostel accommodating many first- and second-year students. Offers shared rooms and a friendly, active environment.

  4. Sultan Bello Hall

    5. Male undergraduate hostel named after Sultan Bello. Involved in inter-hall sports and cultural competitions.

  5. Nnamdi Azikiwe Hall (Zik Hall)

    6. Large male hostel named after Nigeria’s first president. Known for hall events, competitions, and an active student community.
